As a general rule of thumb you should charge the batteries before opening night, but given that the only fast charger we have is only a 4 bay, it's easier said than done. 

Best way to check is to use a multimeter and check the voltage level of the battery itself. Given that everyone I talk to looks at me like I have five heads when the word multimeter comes up, its the black rectangle with orange buttons and the red and black wires sticking out of it. 

To check the voltage level turn the dial until you see volts or turn the dial til it's straight up and then one to the left. From there you hold the end of the red wire to the positive end of the batter and the black wire to the negative end of the battery. It really isn't rocket science.
