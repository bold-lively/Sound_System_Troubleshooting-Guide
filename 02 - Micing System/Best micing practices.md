
- Attach the mic about an inch away from the actors mouth
- Either put a second piece of tape on the back of the actors ear or on their neck depending on there preference 
- Please make sure that the wire is somewhat hidden and not sticking out the back of a costume awkwardly. 
- When making the micing list, assign mics 3/4 to the principal actor/s so that the leads are consistent from show to show
- It can be easier to assign them by gender. (4-7 is the girls, 7-12 is the guys) to make micing easier.
